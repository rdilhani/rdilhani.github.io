# 🎓 **Digital Tools for Smarter Learning and Careers**

---

## 📌 Overview

This 3-hour interactive workshop explores how **Information Technology (IT)**, **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, and **Learning Management Systems (LMS)** are transforming education and careers.

Students from **Accountancy, IT, English, and Project Management** disciplines will gain practical exposure to AI tools, understand LMS platforms, and explore how digital skills can support their learning and future professions.

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants will be able to:

* Identify the role of IT and AI in modern learning and workplaces.
* Use AI-powered tools to support academic and professional tasks.
* Experience how LMS platforms enhance collaboration and assessment.
* Discuss future skills and AI applications in their own discipline.

---

## 📅 Agenda

**1. Introduction & Icebreaker (15 min)**

* Why IT & AI matter for all disciplines

**2. Digital Transformation Across Disciplines (30 min)**

* Case studies in Accountancy, IT, English, and Project Management

**3. Hands-On with AI Tools (45 min)**

* ChatGPT, Grammarly, Excel AI, Trello demo
* Discipline-based group tasks

**4. Learning Management Systems in Action (45 min)**

* Demo: Moodle / Google Classroom
* Activity: Submitting responses online

**5. Future Skills & Brainstorm (30 min)**

* AI applications in each discipline

**6. Wrap-Up & Q\&A (15 min)**

* Recap & sharing resources

---

## 💡 Discipline-Specific Focus

* **Accountancy:** AI in auditing, data visualization, Excel forecasting.
* **IT:** AI code assistants, cybersecurity automation, cloud solutions.
* **English:** AI grammar tools, translation apps, writing assistants.
* **Project Management:** AI-powered planning, task tracking, risk prediction.

### 🔸 Activity 1: AI in Auditing (Fraud Detection Demo)

**Tool:** [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com/) or [Bing Copilot](https://www.bing.com/chat)
**Steps:**

1. Use the dataset (CSV or Excel) showing company expenses (some with suspicious duplicate entries).
2. Paste a sample of the data into ChatGPT and prompt:
   *“Analyze this data and identify unusual or suspicious entries.”*
3. AI will highlight patterns that could represent fraud.
   👉 **Outcome:** Students see how AI helps auditors detect anomalies.

### 🔸 Activity 2: Data Visualization with AI

**Tool:** [Flourish](https://flourish.studio/) (free online visualization tool)
**Steps:**

1. Upload a small Excel sheet with sales or expenses.
2. Choose a visualization (bar chart, pie chart, line graph).
3. Customize the chart → add labels & colors.
4. Share the live visualization link.
   👉 **Outcome:** Students learn how AI-driven platforms simplify visualization.

---

### 🔸 Activity 3: Excel Forecasting with AI Features

**Tool:** [Microsoft Excel](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/excel) (Forecast Sheet feature)
**Steps:**

1. Enter monthly sales data in Excel.
2. Select the data → Click **Data > Forecast Sheet**.
3. Excel auto-generates a **future trend chart**.
   👉 **Outcome:** Students experience how Excel can predict trends using AI-based forecasting.

## **2. IT**

### 🔸 Activity 1: AI Code Assistant

**Tool:** [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot) (free student access) OR [Replit Ghostwriter](https://replit.com/site/ghostwriter)
**Steps:**

1. Open GitHub Copilot or Replit.
2. Type a comment: `// Write a C# program to add two numbers`
3. Watch how AI generates the code automatically.
   👉 **Outcome:** Students learn how AI supports coding productivity.

---

### 🔸 Activity 2: Cybersecurity Automation (Password Strength Check)

**Tool:** [Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com/)
**Steps:**

1. Create a **test password** (not real personal ones).
2. Enter it into the password checker.
3. Show how AI-powered tools evaluate strength and breaches.
   👉 **Outcome:** Students see AI’s role in improving cybersecurity.

---

### 🔸 Activity 3: Cloud AI Services

**Tool:** [Google Cloud AI Demos](https://cloud.google.com/products/ai)
**Steps:**

1. Open demo tools like **Vision AI** or **Translation AI**.
2. Upload an image or text sample.
3. See how the cloud service analyzes/ translates instantly.
   👉 **Outcome:** Students understand AI’s integration in cloud platforms.

### 🔸 Activity 2: Translation Apps

**Tool:** [Google Translate](https://translate.google.lk) or [DeepL Translator](https://www.deepl.com/)
**Steps:**

1. Enter an English sentence (e.g., “Knowledge is power”).
2. Translate it into French, German, or Sinhala.
3. Compare translations with Google Translate for accuracy.
   👉 **Outcome:** Students see AI-powered translations in action.

---

### 🔸 Activity 3: Writing Assistant with AI

**Tool:** [QuillBot](https://quillbot.com/)
**Steps:**

1. Paste a formal academic text.
2. Use **Paraphraser** or **Summarizer** options.
3. Compare AI output with original.
   👉 **Outcome:** Students see how AI can refine writing & generate summaries.

## **4. Project Management**

### 🔸 Activity 1: AI-Powered Planning

**Tool:** [Notion AI](https://www.notion.so/product/ai) (free trial)
**Steps:**

1. Create a new project in Notion (e.g., “College Event”).
2. Ask Notion AI: *“Generate a project plan with milestones.”*
3. AI creates a step-by-step timeline.
   👉 **Outcome:** Students see how AI creates structured project plans.
   
---

### 🔸 Activity 2: Task Tracking

**Tool:** [Trello with AI Power-Up](https://trello.com/)
**Steps:**

1. Create a new board (Project → “Workshop Planning”).
2. Add tasks: “Design poster,” “Book venue,” “Send invitations.”
3. Enable AI Power-Up → Ask AI to prioritize tasks.
   👉 **Outcome:** Students learn how AI helps in task organization.

---

### 🔸 Activity 3: Risk Prediction

**Tool:** [ClickUp AI](https://clickup.com/ai) (trial version)
**Steps:**

1. Create a sample project.
2. Add a task with dependencies (e.g., “Printing flyers before event”).
3. Ask AI: *“What risks may delay this project?”*
4. AI suggests risks (printing delays, approvals, deadlines).
   👉 **Outcome:** Students understand predictive analytics in project management.
