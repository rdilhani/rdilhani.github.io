# 🎓 **Digital Tools for Smarter Learning and Careers**

---

## 📌 Overview

This 3-hour interactive workshop explores how **Information Technology (IT)**, **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, and **Learning Management Systems (LMS)** are transforming education and careers.

Students from **Accountancy, IT, English, and Project Management** disciplines will gain practical exposure to AI tools, understand LMS platforms, and explore how digital skills can support their learning and future professions.

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants will be able to:

* Identify the role of IT and AI in modern learning and workplaces.
* Use AI-powered tools to support academic and professional tasks.
* Experience how LMS platforms enhance collaboration and assessment.
* Discuss future skills and AI applications in their own discipline.

---

## 📅 Agenda

**1. Introduction & Icebreaker (15 min)**

* Why IT & AI matter for all disciplines

**2. Digital Transformation Across Disciplines (30 min)**

* Case studies in Accountancy, IT, English, and Project Management

**3. Hands-On with AI Tools (45 min)**

* ChatGPT, Grammarly, Excel AI, Trello demo
* Discipline-based group tasks

**4. Learning Management Systems in Action (45 min)**

* Demo: Moodle / Google Classroom
* Activity: Submitting responses online

**5. Future Skills & Brainstorm (30 min)**

* AI applications in each discipline

**6. Wrap-Up & Q\&A (15 min)**

* Recap & sharing resources

---

## 💡 Discipline-Specific Focus

* **Accountancy:** AI in auditing, data visualization, Excel forecasting.
* **IT:** AI code assistants, cybersecurity automation, cloud solutions.
* **English:** AI grammar tools, translation apps, writing assistants.
* **Project Management:** AI-powered planning, task tracking, risk prediction.
